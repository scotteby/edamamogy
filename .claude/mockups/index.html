Etymology — Convivial
The Latin root 'vivere' meaning to live is remarkably productive in English: 'vivid' means full of life, 'revive' means to bring back to life, and 'vivacious' describes someone brimming with energy. The prefix 'con' meaning together also appears in 'converse' and 'congregate,' so convivial literally means 'living it up together.'
